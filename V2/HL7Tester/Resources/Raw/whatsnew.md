## v2.0.27

### 🔌 Check If Your Server Is Reachable
> Know right away whether your HL7 server is up and responding.
- A one-tap "Test" button on every saved connection
- A green badge confirms the server is reachable
- A red badge tells you it isn't
- Instant results, no guessing

### 🟢 Live Status Right on the Home Screen
> A small indicator shows whether your server is online.
- See your environment name and its status at a glance
- Online or offline, shown clearly
- Refreshes on its own every minute
- No need to check manually
