## v2.0.28

### 🧍 Run a Full Scenario on One Patient
> Rehearse a complete care flow on a single, fixed patient instead of random ones.
- Turn on "Unique patient flow" to keep the same patient across every step
- Set Patient number, Admission number, First name and Last name yourself
- Works great for replaying real-world admission to discharge sequences

### 🧩 Batch Send Settings, Side by Side
> The Batch Send screen is cleaner and easier to scan.
- Common settings now sit right next to the workflow templates
- Room, bed, unit and floor settings live under one clear "Common settings" card

### 🔌 Connection Kept Open by Default
> Your connection stays open between messages without any extra click.
- The keep-connection option now starts switched on

### 🐛 Batch Steps Show Up on Mac
> Your step list now displays correctly on macOS.
- Steps appear right after you add a workflow template on Mac as well as Windows
